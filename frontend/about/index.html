About PrepSpace

Building a Better Way to Prepare, Discover, and Connect.

PrepSpace is an enterprise-grade developer career intelligence and technical interview readiness platform. We bridge the gap between theoretical computer science concepts, structured coding practice, timed technical assessments, and transparent job pipeline tracking.

Explore Platform Our Capabilities

Empowering the Next Generation of Software Engineers

Preparing for high-stakes technical interviews at modern technology companies has historically been fragmented, opaque, and stressful. Candidates often juggle disconnected problem lists, arbitrary online timers, unstructured study notes, and untracked job spreadsheets.

Stream-In was engineered to solve this challenge by consolidating the entire technical career preparation lifecycle into a single, cohesive, high-performance workspace. From foundational Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA) roadmaps to automated 50-MCQ timed mock exams and Kanban placement boards, we turn preparation into a measurable science.

Our Mission

To democratize high-yield technical interview preparation through accessible, transparent, and data-driven learning tools. We believe that mastery of technical problem-solving should be guided by clear milestones, immediate system feedback, and responsible software engineering practices.

Our Vision

To become the global standard platform where developers continuously benchmark their technical knowledge, showcase verified credentials, and navigate their entire career progression with confidence and clarity.

Core Product Capabilities

What Stream-In Delivers

Explore the production features engineered into the Stream-In platform to accelerate your technical interview readiness.

Automated 50-MCQ Assessments

System-generated 10 to 50 multiple choice questions across DSA, Java, SQL, OS, CN, and Python with automated grading, instant scorecards, and explanatory reviews.

Unified Career Intelligence Dashboard

Interactive analytics charts measuring weekly study time, difficulty distributions, problem-solving streaks, and comprehensive interview readiness indices.

LeetCode Problem Workspace

Curated coding challenge repositories filtered by company tags and topic hierarchies with integrated approach guides, hints, and personal note saving.

Curriculum & Certification Center

Structured multi-lesson video tracks with milestone quiz validation and official digital completion certificates ready for professional profiles.

Kanban Job Pipeline Tracker

Visual pipeline tracking for applications across Applied, Screening, Technical, Final Round, and Offer stages with compensation milestones.

AI Diagnostics & Study Planners

Automated ATS resume compliance checks, algorithmic weak-topic diagnostic reports, and company-specific interview preparation prompts.
