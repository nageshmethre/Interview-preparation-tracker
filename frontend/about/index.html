What is PrepSpace?

PrepSpace is the ultimate technical interview preparation tracker SaaS for software developers. The platform is designed to streamline your tech recruitment prep by tracking your progress across Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA), System Design, Mock Exams, and Job Application lifecycles—all inside a single premium visual dashboard.

Key Features

  • DSA Trackers: Checklists and progress indicators for popular DSA problems.
  • Mock Exams: Set timers and take mock evaluations to analyze technical readiness.
  • Job Application Board: Kanban boards to pipeline and keep track of application phases.
  • Study Planner: Customize study schedules with an interactive calendar.
