Summary Overview: Stream-In respects your privacy. We collect only the information necessary to provide our career preparation tracking, mock examinations, and dashboard analytics. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Introduction & Operator

This Privacy Policy describes how Stream-In (also operating as PrepSpace, accessible via https://stream-in.app) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information.

By accessing, registering with, or utilizing any feature of the Stream-In platform, you acknowledge the data collection, processing, and disclosure practices outlined in this policy. For inquiries, you may reach us at support@stream-in.app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide the platform's career preparation services:

2.1 Information You Directly Provide

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, encrypted password hash (processed via BCrypt).
  • Profile & Learning Preferences: Target tech companies, preferred programming languages, daily study goals, salary targets, and theme preferences.
  • User-Generated Study Content: Study notes, markdown documents, bookmark tags, personal problem hints, and interview experience postings.
  • Placement Funnels: Company names, job application stages (Applied, Screening, Technical, Final, Offer), interview dates, and compensation details logged on your Kanban board.

2.2 Assessment & Telemetry Data

  • Assessment Metrics: Multiple-Choice Question (MCQ) answers, test timestamps, examination scores, accuracy percentages, earned XP points, and streak counts.
  • Coding Activity: Problem completion timestamps, solved difficulty tiers (Easy, Medium, Hard), and study session durations.

2.3 Payment & Transaction Information

When purchasing the PrepPro tier (₹99 one-time access), payments are processed by our PCI-DSS compliant payment partner, Razorpay. Stream-In records only transaction identifiers, order IDs, timestamps, and verification signatures. We never store, access, or handle raw credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs on our servers.

3. How We Collect Data

  • Direct Interactions: Data you input when filling registration forms, staging mock exams, updating study planners, or submitting support inquiries.
  • Third-Party Authentication: When opting to sign in via Google OAuth2, we receive your verified name, email address, and Google account identifier.
  • Automated Local Storage: Client session tokens (`token`), active theme configurations (`theme`), and UI preferences stored directly in your browser's Local Storage.
  • Server Log Telemetry: Standard HTTP request headers, IP address, user-agent string, and API latency measurements.

4. How We Use Information

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Maintaining candidate accounts, rendering customized dashboards, and executing timed 50-MCQ exams.
  • Automated Grading & Analytics: Comparing candidate test answers against key indices, computing readiness percentages, and awarding streak XP.
  • Security & Authentication: Verifying JWT bearer tokens, authenticating API endpoints, and preventing brute-force attacks.
  • Transaction Processing: Validating Razorpay cryptographic payment signatures and activating PrepPro access.
  • Platform Diagnostics: Resolving application errors, optimizing API latency, and auditing database performance.
  • Legal Compliance: Complying with tax regulations, accounting audits, and applicable statutory laws.

6. Third-Party Integrations

Stream-In integrates with verified technology service providers:

  • Google Identity Services: Facilitating optional one-tap Single Sign-On (SSO).
  • Razorpay Payment Gateway: Processing order creation and payment authorization.
  • YouTube Embedded Video Player: Streaming curriculum video lessons securely.
  • Google AdSense: Providing display monetization on public pages.
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Vercel (Edge CDN frontend) and Render (Secure backend container hosting).

7. Data Sharing & Non-Sale Statement

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information with third-party data brokers or marketing agencies.

Information is disclosed only: (a) to operational infrastructure vendors under confidentiality agreements, (b) to comply with lawful subpoenas, court orders, or applicable regulations, or (c) in connection with a corporate merger, acquisition, or asset reorganization.

8. Technical & Data Security

We implement industry-aligned organizational and technical safeguards:

  • Encryption in Transit: 100% of platform traffic is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS 1.3.
  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: Passwords are irreversibly hashed using BCrypt before persistence.
  • Stateless JWT Authentication: API access is guarded by cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens.
  • Least-Privilege Database Access: Backend services operate on isolated database clusters with restricted network firewalls.

9. Data Retention & Erasure

We retain account data for as long as your profile remains active. You may request immediate and permanent deletion of your account, notes, exam history, and personal information at any time by contacting support@stream-in.app. Upon verification, data is purged from our production databases within 30 days.

10. User Rights & Choices

Regardless of your jurisdiction, Stream-In affords you comprehensive privacy rights:

  • Right to Access & Review: View all logged practice problems, notes, and profile details on your dashboard.
  • Right to Rectification: Update your profile name, password, and career targets via Settings.
  • Right to Data Portability: Export candidate progress records and application logs as Excel / PDF files.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent removal of your account and personal records.

11. Children's Privacy

Stream-In is intended exclusively for software engineering candidates, students, and professionals aged 13 and older (or 16 where required by local jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a minor has registered an account without guardian consent, please contact us for immediate deletion.

12. International Data Transfers

Your information may be stored and processed on servers located in various cloud availability regions maintained by Vercel and Render. By using Stream-In, you acknowledge that international data transmissions are protected under standard contractual clauses and robust encryption standards.

13. Incident Response & Breach Notification

In the event of a confirmed security incident impacting personal data, Stream-In maintains an incident response procedure to contain the breach, notify affected users via email, and report to regulatory authorities in compliance with applicable laws.

14. Policy Revisions

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in platform functionality or regulatory changes. Significant updates will be noted with a revised "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of Stream-In constitutes acceptance of the amended terms.
